Awareness

  • [Portfolio | Grind] Iran received the US reply to its Hormuz plan after a new threat (30 Sep). The sides differ on sequencing a 7-day plan, according to Khaleej Times. On 29 Sep parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said no regional infrastructure would be safe without guarantees for Iran. Implication: Possible escalation trigger. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps named US university campuses in the Gulf as targets in March; NYUAD should keep remote teaching ready. Khaleej Times, 30 Sep
  • [OX] Tencent agreed to lease about 100,000 artificial intelligence (AI) chips from Oracle (30 Sep). The 5-year, $7bn deal covers Oracle data centres in Southeast Asia, according to the Financial Times. Implication: The deal strengthens the Senate case for the Remote Access Security Act, which the House passed on 12 Jan. If enacted, Chinese-linked users of US chips abroad, including in Abu Dhabi, would need licences, narrowing THUAD's computing options. TrendForce, 1 Oct
  • [OX] DeepSeek released open-source tools for Huawei's Ascend AI chips (30 Sep). The package includes TileLang, a Chinese alternative to Nvidia's programming platform, tuned for Ascend 950 chips, according to Bloomberg. Implication: Huawei hardware becomes easier to use outside China. G42 removed Huawei equipment in 2024 before winning US chip approvals in Nov 2025, so THUAD needs a stated choice between US-licensed chips and a Huawei stack before announcement. Bloomberg, 30 Sep
  • [Portfolio] The UK Security Service, MI5, told universities to end work with the China General Technology Research Institute (30 Sep). MI5 said the institute funded over 100 academics in AI, cybersecurity and covert communications for China's Ministry of State Security, according to United Press International. Implication: UK partners of NYUAD and MBZUAI will tighten screening of Chinese-funded work, and THUAD's AI partners will face the same checks. United Press International, 30 Sep
  • [OX] Sabato's Crystal Ball moved 11 House races towards Democrats (29 Sep). It rates 213 seats at least leaning Democratic, 5 short of a majority, against 202 leaning Republican and 20 toss-ups. Implication: A Democratic House would chair the Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party and the Education and Workforce Committee from January 2027, so THUAD's first year after a November announcement would face new committee leadership. Sabato's Crystal Ball, 29 Sep
